Clipboard access

Risk warning

When enabled, DSH can read everything currently on your system clipboard — which may include passwords, tokens, account numbers, or other private data — and can place text on the clipboard and paste it into pages with a real Ctrl+V. Only enable this if you understand and accept these risks. Keep it off unless you need it.
