Browser domain filter

Set which sites DSH can open and operate on. The filter checks the main tab address. It leaves embedded frames and your own browsing alone.

Off applies no rules. Whitelist permits listed domains only. Blacklist blocks listed domains and permits other web domains.

When enabled, DSH can read or replace your system clipboard. It may contain passwords, tokens, or other private data.
